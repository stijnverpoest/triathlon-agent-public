# Andrew Coggan Bike Doctrine

Use Coggan-style bike logic for all structured ride sessions.

## Allowed level vocabulary

- `L1 Recovery`
- `L2 Endurance`
- `L3 Tempo`
- `L4 Threshold`
- `L5 VO2max`
- `L6 Anaerobic`
- `L7 Neuromuscular`

## Rules

- every ride must declare a primary Coggan level
- every structured ride should declare expected `IF` and estimated `TSS`
- deliberate L3 (tempo / sweet spot) and L4 work is the backbone of a pyramidal long-course week; avoid only unplanned drift on L2 rides
- long-course weeks bias `L2`, `L3`, and `L4`, with `L5` VO2max blocks (3-4 weeks) from build onward or when eFTP stagnates
- 70.3 long rides in build / race-specific phases carry a race-power block late in the ride (IF ~0.75-0.82 race intensity)
- progress quality rides along the ladders in `training_intensity_doctrine.md`; record the rung on the session
- the bike week should be explainable in terms of stress progression, not just workout novelty
